Who We Are

A foundation built on action

Pan Africa Vision Foundation (PAVF) is a Pan-African development and empowerment foundation committed to transforming lives, strengthening communities and creating opportunities for Africans, particularly young people and vulnerable populations.

We combine compassion with action, knowledge with opportunity, and dialogue with measurable impact.

Africa's transformation is not the responsibility of governments and institutions alone. Every African has a role to play.
