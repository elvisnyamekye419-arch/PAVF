Endless Impact Seminar Series

Where knowledge meets action: inspiring, educating and empowering the next generation of African leaders.

Seminar 1.0

The Intersection of Knowledge and Action

19 April 2025 Faculty of Law Auditorium, UDS Nyankpala Campus 70 participants

The inaugural Endless Impact Seminar brought participants together at the University for Development Studies, Nyankpala Campus, to explore how knowledge, ideas and conversations can be turned into practical action. Refreshments were served throughout the event.

The seminar continued PAVF's founding philosophy: conversations should not end with conversation; they should inspire action.

Seminar 2.0

Inspiring, Educating, Empowering the Next Generation of Leaders

11 November 2025 Tamale Girls Senior High School 1,000+ students

The second major edition brought together more than 1,000 students for an engagement on the future of young Africans, with speakers and the active participation of school authorities.

The programme exposed students to ideas, experiences and perspectives to inspire excellence and leadership. It also featured citations and awards recognizing individuals whose contributions reflected the values of excellence, service and positive impact.
